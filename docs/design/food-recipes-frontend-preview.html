WEEKNIGHT FAVORITE

ONE PAN / 30 MINUTES

Lemon herb tray bake

Bright vegetables and golden chicken come together on one easy pan. A reliable dinner for the evenings when you want something good without a long decision tree.

★★★★★ 4.8 from 126 cooks
30 minTotal time
EasySkill level
4Servings
Start cooking

Ingredients

  • Chicken thighs4
  • Baby potatoes500 g
  • Green beans200 g
  • Lemon + herbsto taste

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C and line one large tray.
  2. Toss the vegetables with olive oil, salt, and pepper.
  3. Add the chicken, lemon, and herbs, then roast until golden.
