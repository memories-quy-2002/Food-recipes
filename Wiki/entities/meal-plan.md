# Meal plan

A meal plan groups recipes or eligible leftovers by date and meal slot. Each plan belongs to one user account. Related records support plan items,
templates, template items, and recurring rules. New food preference records
default to one serving; existing preference values are retained. Planning services define
validation and connections to shopping and cooking.
